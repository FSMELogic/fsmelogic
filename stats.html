Google Analytics — Real Cross-Device Data

For accurate, cross-device download tracking: Every store interaction fires a GA4 event. To see real-world data:

1. Go to analytics.google.com
2. Select your FSME Logic property (G-H3ZFCJGRSS)
3. Navigate to Reports → Engagement → Events
4. Look for: file_download (free kit clicks) and begin_checkout (paid product clicks)

The localStorage log on this page only captures activity from visitors using this specific browser on this device — useful for testing, but GA is the source of truth for real traffic.
