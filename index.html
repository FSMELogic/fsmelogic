The Problem

Why did my equipment fail without a threshold alarm?

Equipment often fails without triggering a threshold alarm because traditional monitoring systems rely on the "Green Dashboard Fallacy." Standard sensors only alert operators when physical damage has already crossed a critical redline. They are designed to measure acute damage, not the hidden structural stress accumulating over time.

Your operations rely on early warnings, but threshold-based SCADA systems wait until a catastrophic event is actively occurring to notify you. By the time a vibration sensor spikes or a thermal gauge alerts your team, the timeline for preventative maintenance has passed. You are no longer preventing a failure; you are managing a breakdown.

Predictive Maintenance Beyond Threshold Sensors

FSME Logic solves this by analyzing the causal memory of the system. Instead of waiting for a single metric to break a threshold limit, our offline physics engine analyzes the complex, multi-variable interactions within your existing sensor data. We identify the microscopic degradation of your assets weeks or months in advance, allowing you to schedule repairs on your timeline, entirely eliminating unplanned downtime.

The Solution

How does offline predictive maintenance work?

Offline predictive maintenance analyzes critical industrial telemetry on localized, air-gapped edge hardware. This allows defense contractors and heavy industrial operators to detect equipment failure using advanced physics engines without ever uploading sensitive SCADA or proprietary telemetry data to a cloud network.

Legacy AI solutions require you to export massive volumes of your operational data to external servers, creating unacceptable security risks for aerospace, utilities, and commercial fleets. FSME Logic provides absolute data sovereignty.

Zero Data Exit Audits

We deploy our proprietary engine directly to your facility. Our ingestion tools process your existing CSV, HDF5, or TDMS telemetry exports locally. You receive a comprehensive forensic diagnostic report and a cryptographic SHA-256 custody receipt proving that not a single byte of your operational data left your facility.

No internet connection. No third-party cloud processing. No training data required.
