Section 06

Data Handling & Security Architecture

The FSME Logic engine is designed from the ground up as an air-gapped, offline signal processor. Its architecture carries specific and significant security implications for clients.

  • No PII processing: The engine does not collect, store, process, or transmit Personally Identifiable Information (PII) by design. Client operational telemetry is processed as anonymous numerical time-series data.
  • No cloud transmission: Client data submitted for analysis is processed entirely on local, offline, air-gapped hardware. It is not transmitted over any network, uploaded to any cloud service, or processed by any third-party platform at any stage of an engagement.
  • SHA-256 custody: Every engagement produces a SHA-256 cryptographic chain of custody receipt documenting the data hash at intake and confirming that the dataset was not modified, copied, or transmitted during processing.
  • Zero security incidents: FSME Logic has a verifiable record of zero security incidents, data breaches, or unauthorized disclosures.
  • Regulatory posture: The zero-PII, air-gapped architecture substantially reduces standard cybersecurity and data protection regulatory compliance burdens for enterprise clients operating under PIPEDA, GDPR, ITAR, or sector-specific data governance frameworks.
