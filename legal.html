Section 01

Corporate Governance & Entity Structure

FSME Logic operates as a registered sole proprietorship under the jurisdiction of Alberta, Canada, operated by Alexander Kalyniuk as the sole founder and equity holder.

  • Clean capitalization: There is a single equity holder. There are zero outstanding warrants, options, convertible notes, or shadow equity agreements of any kind.
  • No litigation: FSME Logic has no pending, threatened, or active litigation, arbitration, regulatory investigation, or administrative proceedings.
  • No encumbrances: The company operates free of any liens, security interests, or third-party claims against its intellectual property or commercial assets.
  • Transaction structure: The corporate framework is optimized for asset purchase agreements. Any acquiring entity or exclusive licensee assumes no legacy corporate liabilities through an asset transaction.

Section 02

Intellectual Property & Chain of Title

One hundred percent (100%) of the intellectual property underlying FSME Logic — including all foundational methodology, signal processing architecture, and detection logic — is held personally and exclusively by the founder, Alexander Kalyniuk.

  • Sole authorship: The entire software architecture was authored exclusively by the founder. Zero third-party contractors, overseas developers, or external contributors were involved at any stage. The chain of title is unbroken and unencumbered.
  • Patent protection: The core detection methodology is actively protected under Canadian Patent Application No. 3,297,487 with a secured priority date of January 1, 2026. All patent rights are held solely by the founder.
  • Defensive prior art: A defensive publication record has been established to prevent competing entities from independently patenting the foundational theoretical mechanisms.
  • Copyright: All website content, documentation, reports, forensic output formats, and marketing materials are copyright © 2026 Alexander Kalyniuk. All rights reserved.

Section 03

Trade Secrets & Calibration Methodology

Beyond formal patent protection, FSME Logic's commercial advantage is further defended by a body of trade secrets that are not disclosed in any public filing, publication, or website.

The specific hardware-calibrated operational parameters that make the engine commercially functional are held as protected trade secrets. While the foundational physics are established in public prior art, the precise calibration methodology is not publicly disclosed and is protected under applicable Canadian trade secret law.

Any reverse engineering, disassembly, decompilation, or attempted reconstruction of proprietary calibration parameters from FSME Logic's public outputs, forensic reports, or marketing materials is expressly prohibited and constitutes misappropriation of trade secrets.

The proprietary source code is maintained in offline, air-gapped cold storage, mitigating risk of unauthorized access or cloud-based exposure.

Section 04

Software Licensing & Open-Source Compliance

  • Proprietary core: The core mathematical processing engine is built on strictly proprietary architecture. No portion of the core processing logic is derived from any open-source licensed component.
  • Permissive dependencies only: Operational wrappers utilize standard open-source libraries under permissive licenses (MIT and Apache 2.0) only. The proprietary binary is entirely free from copyleft (GPL, LGPL, AGPL) contamination.
  • No copyleft exposure: No component of the commercially licensed software requires licensees to disclose, open-source, or share modifications to any portion of their technology stack.

Section 05

Commercial Scope & Field of Use

All commercial licensing agreements are strictly bounded by defined Fields of Use. No single agreement grants rights across all potential application domains unless explicitly negotiated in a separate written instrument. Current and anticipated Fields of Use include:

  • Industrial equipment integrity monitoring and fleet failure detection
  • Aerospace and defense telemetry analysis
  • Quantum computing hardware diagnostics and calibration triggering
  • Financial market signal integrity and anomaly detection
  • Cold chain and refrigeration system monitoring

The founder expressly retains all overarching, royalty-free rights for academic research, scientific publication, and non-commercial theoretical physics investigation, regardless of any commercial licensing arrangement.

Section 06

Data Handling & Security Architecture

  • No PII processing: The engine does not collect, store, process, or transmit Personally Identifiable Information by design. Client telemetry is processed as anonymous numerical time-series data.
  • No cloud transmission: Client data is processed entirely on local, offline, air-gapped hardware. It is not transmitted over any network, uploaded to any cloud service, or processed by any third-party platform.
  • SHA-256 custody: Every engagement produces a SHA-256 cryptographic chain of custody receipt confirming the data hash at intake and confirming secure, unmodified handling.
  • Zero security incidents: FSME Logic has a verifiable record of zero security incidents, data breaches, or unauthorized disclosures.
  • Regulatory posture: The zero-PII, air-gapped architecture substantially reduces compliance burdens for enterprise clients operating under PIPEDA, GDPR, ITAR, or sector-specific data governance frameworks.
