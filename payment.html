FSME Logic Integrity Audit

On-site predictive integrity audit of your equipment or fleet telemetry. Air-gapped, offline hardware — your data is handled on air-gapped, offline hardware.

Deliverables

  • Live analysis across all available sensor channels in your data export
  • Identification of active stress signatures and earliest detection timeline
  • Channel-by-channel health classification — confirmed healthy vs. flagged
  • Plain-language forensic summary with recommended action
  • SHA-256 chain of custody receipt — cryptographic proof of secure data handling
  • No new sensors, hardware, or infrastructure required
  • Works with your existing SCADA, BMS, or data logger CSV exports

// Data Security Guarantee

All analysis is performed on local, offline, air-gapped hardware in Edmonton. Your operational data is never uploaded to any cloud server and never transmitted over any network at any stage of the engagement.

How It Works

01
Export your data — Pull a CSV from your existing monitoring system. 30–90 days of sensor readings is ideal.
02
On-site audit — We run the FSME engine locally on your data. No internet connection. No cloud upload. Analysis completes in minutes.
03
Forensic report — A clear summary identifying which systems showed early warning indicators and what action is recommended.
04
Custody receipt — SHA-256 cryptographic proof of secure data handling delivered at the end of every engagement.
