What to Include in Your Message

  • Your industry and the type of equipment or systems you want assessed
  • Whether you already have sensor data being logged (SCADA, BMS, CSV exports, etc.)
  • What problem you're trying to solve — unplanned downtime, reactive maintenance costs, system reliability
  • Your location and whether on-site or remote engagement fits better

What Happens Next

We'll review your situation, confirm that we can work with your existing data format, and outline an engagement scope — including what the audit will cover and what you'll receive at the end.

Data Security

Your operational data is never uploaded to any cloud server. All analysis is performed on local, offline hardware. For aerospace and defense engagements, fully air-gapped secure analysis is available. Your data stays under your control throughout the entire process.

No Commitment Required

An initial conversation costs nothing. If your data format works and the engagement makes sense for your situation, we'll outline exactly what you'd receive before any commitment is made.
