Data Security

Your operational data is never uploaded to any cloud server. All analysis is performed on local, offline hardware. For aerospace and defense engagements, fully air-gapped on-site analysis is available. Your data stays under your control throughout.

What Happens Next

We'll review your inquiry, confirm that your existing data format is compatible, and outline an engagement scope — including exactly what the audit will cover and what you'll receive. No commitment required at this stage.

What to Have Ready

You don't need anything prepared to start a conversation. When we move toward an audit, we'll typically need a CSV export from your existing monitoring system — 30 to 90 days of historical sensor readings is a solid starting point for most engagements.
