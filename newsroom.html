March 2026 Announcement

FSME Logic Is Launching Commercially This Quarter

After months of focused development, validation, and infrastructure work, FSME Logic is moving into its first commercial phase this quarter. The engine is built, tested, and ready — and we are now actively engaging our first clients.

This marks the transition from a research and validation operation into a fully commercial signal integrity platform. The same technology that has been validated against aerospace telemetry and industrial fleet datasets is now available for engagement by any organization running equipment that generates sensor data.

We will have more to share in the coming weeks — including details on our first client engagements, new validation results, and updates on the company's development roadmap. If you have been following our work or are curious about what FSME Logic can do for your operation, now is a good time to get in touch.

Exciting things ahead. Stay tuned.
