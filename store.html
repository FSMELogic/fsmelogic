AI Governance · Offline

Governor

A checkpoint that sits in front of a local AI. Every request and answer is allowed or blocked by deterministic rules you own, and leaves with an Ed25519-signed record of exactly what happened. Built on the Stratum runtime: refusals are structural, not a filter a clever prompt can talk around.

  • Runs on your hardware, binds to localhost only
  • OpenAI-compatible endpoint: drops into existing tooling
  • Signed, tamper-evident decision records
  • Rules are yours, written in Stratum

What it is not: a promise that a model is "safe." It bounds what the model can do and proves what it did.
