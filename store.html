// About the Technology

FSME Logic provides physics-based Prognostics and Health Management (PHM) software designed for heavy industry, aerospace, and defense contractors. Unlike statistical machine learning platforms that require massive historical training datasets and constant cloud connectivity, the FSME Predictive Integrity Engine utilizes deterministic, first-principles telemetry analysis to identify structural signal degradation weeks before amplitude-based threshold alarms trigger — enabling advanced Condition-Based Maintenance Plus (CBM+) without exposing Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) to third-party cloud vulnerabilities.

Why deterministic physics outperforms statistical ML: The FSME engine requires zero historical failure data, operates 100% offline on air-gapped edge-compute hardware, and is immune to the stochastic hallucinations that plague standard ML anomaly detection. All analysis is conducted on isolated edge hardware in Edmonton, ensuring zero-trust compliance for defense contractors handling export-controlled (ITAR) or CUI telemetry.  Vendor & Procurement Info
