What does FSME Logic actually measure? +
FSME Logic characterizes the entropic behavior of hardware sensor streams — specifically how signal complexity evolves over time as mechanical systems degrade. Deviations from baseline stiffness patterns precede physical failure by weeks to months, enabling intervention before breakdown.
Do you require cloud access to my equipment data? +
No. FSME Logic operates entirely on client-provided data files. Nothing is transmitted to external servers during analysis. The audit engine runs on air-gapped or offline hardware where required by client policy.
What industries have you validated against? +
Aviation, aerospace, satellite operations, oil and gas pipeline, long-haul trucking, commercial refrigeration, and scientific instrumentation. The underlying signal characterization method is domain-agnostic.
How is the patent pending protecting the methodology? +
CA Patent Application 3297487 covers the hardware entropy characterization and predictive maintenance methodology. Signal conditioning and calibration implementation details are maintained as trade secrets separate from any publication.
